01 / About

Engineering first.
AI where it helps.

My background is in computer science and backend engineering. I’m most comfortable working on the parts of a product that need careful reasoning: architecture, APIs, data, reliability, and the boundaries between systems.

I’m now applying that foundation to AI-assisted products and developer tools. I don’t treat AI as the answer to every problem. I’m interested in where it creates genuine leverage—and in building the surrounding software that keeps it understandable, testable, and useful.

On the frontend, I use AI-assisted development to move quickly from an idea to a working interface, then refine the result with human judgment. The goal is not more software. It is a clearer solution.
